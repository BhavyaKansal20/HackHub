# HackHub Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of HackHub pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy builder community at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET) and beyond.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for HackHub:

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward fellow student builders and teammates.
* **Being respectful of differing opinions**, technical approaches, and experiences.
* **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback** on pull requests and hackathon ideas.
* **Accepting responsibility and apologizing** to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* **Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals**, but for the overall HackHub developer ecosystem.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical addresses or unapproved contact details, without explicit permission.
* Dishonest conduct in hackathon listings, such as posting malicious links or misrepresenting event terms.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or collegiate setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, discussions, and linked Discord or WhatsApp builder groups), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the HackHub community in public spaces.

## Reporting & Contact

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the HackHub administration team at **multimodexai@gmail.com** or directly to **Bhavya Kansal**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
